# Validation: 1.1.0-paper-optimization

The default bundled NGs experiment was executed ten times with MATLAB R2024b Update 3 on 2026-11-07. Every repetition reran representation learning and final clustering with its recorded seeds, on the same missing-view pattern. The full economy-SVD clustering representation was retained.

All ten executions stopped after 29 iterations with `primal_tolerance`; the maximum final elementwise primal residual was 7.59828261e-07, below `tolPrimal=1e-6`. This is the manuscript's feasibility stopping criterion and is not a stationarity certificate.

All ten partitions were identical up to label permutation. Floating-point-level standard deviations are retained in the machine-readable records. A separate evaluation implementation recomputed all 70 metric values and checked their means and sample deviations. The verification also checked retained SVD subspaces, frozen-surrogate optimality diagnostics, the first qualifying primal stopping iteration, penalty order, masked weight optimality, simplex feasibility and orthogonality.

`tests.json` records all ten passing regression groups, including the five paper-alignment behavior checks. `default_NGs_verification.json` provides the per-run verification records. `NGs_protocol.json`, `NGs_per_run.csv`, and `NGs_summary.csv` describe this version's run. The complete models and labels are generated by `run_demo` under ignored `results/`.

Earlier 1.0.0 records are preserved in `archive/1.0.0-revision/`. These new validation records do not replace or relabel any historical manuscript table.
